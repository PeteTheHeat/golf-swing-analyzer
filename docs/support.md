# Replay Caddie Support

Replay Caddie analyzes selected golf videos locally on an iPhone. It does not
have an account system or access to remotely recover a deleted swing.

## Get help

Open a
[GitHub support issue](https://github.com/PeteTheHeat/golf-swing-analyzer/issues/new)
for a bug, question, or feature request. Include:

- the app version and build shown at the bottom of Settings;
- the iPhone model and iOS version;
- the step that failed and the text of any error; and
- whether the video is portrait or landscape and face-on or down-the-line.

Do not attach a private golf video, analysis report, device identifier, or other
personal information to a public issue.

## Common fixes

- Keep the app open while a full-quality iCloud video downloads and while
  on-device analysis runs.
- Use a video with one golfer visible from head to feet and a stable camera.
- If automatic swing discovery finds no clip, use the manual trim option.
- If storage is unavailable, free device space, restart the app, and try again.

## Delete local data

Delete a swing from the Library to remove its analysis and any private video
copy that no other saved swing uses. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining
app data from the device.

Read the [privacy policy](https://github.com/PeteTheHeat/golf-swing-analyzer/blob/main/docs/privacy.md)
for more detail.
