# Replay Caddie Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Replay Caddie is designed to analyze golf videos on the iPhone.
It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics,
tracking, or third-party software development kits.

## Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal data. Video analysis, body-pose
estimation, swing measurements, and coaching feedback run on the device.

## Photos and video

The app uses Apple's system Photos picker. It can access only the video that a
person explicitly selects. The selected video is copied into the app's private
storage so that it can be analyzed locally. The original remains in Photos.

## Storage and deletion

Imported videos and analysis results remain on the device. The app excludes its
working data from device backups. Deleting a saved swing removes its analysis
and removes the app's private video copy when no other saved swing uses that
copy. Deleting the app removes all of its remaining local data.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share personal data. It does not send golf
videos or analysis results to the developer or to an external AI service.

## Changes

If the app later adds an account, cloud sync, analytics, or another service that
changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will
be updated before that feature is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the public
[support page](https://github.com/PeteTheHeat/golf-swing-analyzer/blob/main/docs/support.md).
Do not include a private video or other personal information in a public issue.
